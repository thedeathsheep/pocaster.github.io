# Claude-compatible Client Instructions

Use `../AGENTS.md` as the authoritative workflow. Read `../PROJECT.md` and `../CURRENT.md` first; read `../STYLE.md` for public-facing content or design work.

Delegated work requires a root `TASK.md`. Follow its scope and acceptance criteria exactly. Do not infer permission to commit, push, deploy, expose private material, or modify unrelated files.

At completion, report changed files, verification performed, and unresolved issues in a compact handoff.
